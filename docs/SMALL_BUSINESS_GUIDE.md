# Noredaz Industries - Small Business Guide (1-5 Employees)

**Wisconsin-first planning guide. Current as of October 5, 2026.**

This document is a compact operating reference for forming Noredaz Industries LLC, hiring the first employees, handling payroll/tax/insurance basics, and adding employees in other states.

## 1. Formation

- Wisconsin domestic LLC online filing: **$130**
- Wisconsin domestic LLC online annual report: **$25/year**
- Wisconsin online LLC dissolution/termination: **$20**
- Federal EIN: **free directly from the IRS**
- Create an operating agreement, dedicated bank account, and bookkeeping system immediately.

Official links:
- Wisconsin DFI fees: https://dfi.wi.gov/Pages/BusinessServices/BusinessEntities/Fees.aspx
- Wisconsin LLC online filing: https://apps.dfi.wi.gov/apps/CorpFormation/directions.aspx?type=12
- IRS EIN: https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/how-to-get-an-employer-identification-number-for-your-business

## 2. Wisconsin tax + payroll baseline

- Wisconsin Business Tax Registration: **$20 initial**, **$10 renewal**.
- Register Wisconsin withholding before/when paying Wisconsin wages.
- 2026 employer FICA baseline: **6.2% Social Security + 1.45% Medicare = 7.65%** of covered wages.
- Use a payroll provider rather than manually calculating deposits and filings.

Official links:
- Wisconsin Business Tax Registration: https://www.revenue.wi.gov/Pages/faqs/pcs-btr.aspx
- Wisconsin withholding: https://www.revenue.wi.gov/Pages/FAQS/pcs-with.aspx
- IRS Publication 15: https://www.irs.gov/publications/p15

## 4. Wisconsin workers' compensation

Wisconsin requires workers' compensation when either:

1. the employer has **3 or more full- or part-time employees**, or
2. one or more employees have been paid **$500 or more in combined gross wages in a calendar quarter for work done in Wisconsin**.

Under the wage trigger, coverage is due by the **10th day of the first month of the next calendar quarter**.

Official links:
- https://dwd.wisconsin.gov/dwd/publications/wc/wkc-13328-p.htm
- https://www.dwd.wisconsin.gov/wc/employers/

Other insurance to consider as Noredaz grows:
- general liability
- technology E&O / professional liability
- hired/non-owned auto liability when employees drive personal vehicles for company work

## 5. Wisconsin unemployment insurance

A commercial Wisconsin employer generally becomes liable when it:

- pays **$1,500 or more in wages in a quarter**, or
- employs at least one person in **20 or more weeks** in a calendar year.

For 2026, Wisconsin lists a **3.05% new-employer rate** for non-construction employers under $500,000 payroll, applied to the first **$14,000** of wages per employee.

Links:
- https://dwd.wisconsin.gov/ui201/t1201.htm
- https://dwd.wisconsin.gov/ui/employers/prior/taxrates-2026.htm

## 6. Out-of-state employees

Treat every new employee work state as a new compliance event before the employee starts.

Check:
- [ ] foreign qualification / state business registration
- [ ] state/local payroll withholding
- [ ] state unemployment registration
- [ ] workers' compensation coverage for that state
- [ ] state/local wage, overtime, leave, pay-frequency and final-pay rules
- [ ] required notices/posters
- [ ] tax nexus consequences with a CPA
- [ ] payroll provider supports the state

SBA expansion guidance:
https://www.sba.gov/counseling/grow-your-business/

## 7. Sales employee model

Working model for early Noredaz web/hosting sales:

- flexible part-time **W-2 employee**
- no equity
- no exclusivity
- employee can work elsewhere
- self-selected availability within company limits
- hourly/base pay if desired
- commission paid under a written plan
- transportation can be walking, public transit, rideshare, bicycle, or car if driving is not an essential job duty
- define exactly when commission is earned and what happens with cancellations/refunds
- salesperson cannot change legal terms, promise unsupported functionality, or bind Noredaz to custom pricing without approval

## 9. Recurring maintenance

**Every payroll**
- approve hours and commission
- review payroll before submission
- reconcile payroll to business bank activity

**Monthly**
- reconcile bank/card accounts
- review subscriptions and hosting/cloud spend
- review receivables and commissions
- maintain a cash/tax reserve

**Quarterly**
- review payroll tax filings and Wisconsin UI/withholding reports
- review workers' comp payroll estimate
- review P&L and sales pipeline

**Annually**
- Wisconsin LLC annual report
- payroll year-end / W-2 work
- insurance renewals
- federal/state business tax returns
- update job, commission and operating policies
